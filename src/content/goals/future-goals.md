title: Future Goals
---

Over the next five years I want to become someone who can design, build, and deploy computer vision and perception systems on real robots.
In the pursuit of knowledge, I have a 1 year learning roadmap setup filled with research papers and projects dedicated to teaching me different concepts.
Split into three tiers, I plan to build a strong foundation of perception primitives, integrate them into a unified autonomous driving perception stack, then deploy that stack as a real-time C++ / ROS2 node on my own robot hardware and measure how it holds up.
This project plan was designed to provide me foundational knowledge and experience in developing perception systems and in getting them running outside a notebook. The full roadmap is outlined in the [Future Goals](/roadmap/#current) timeline.
