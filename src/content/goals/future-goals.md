---
title: Future Goals
---

My goal is to build robots that learn to understand and act in the real world, not just in a single task or a single sensor modality.
Over the next five years I want to become an engineer who can take a robot from raw sensing all the way to intelligent behaviour: perception, learned policies, control, and the infrastructure that makes all of it reliable enough to deploy.
I'm most drawn to where machine learning meets physical hardware. That means vision-language-action models that turn instructions into motion, reinforcement learning that holds up on a real plant, and the evaluation work that tells you whether a robot can actually be trusted.
Every project I take on now is a step toward that, and each one is built to be measured, not just demoed. You can see what I'm working on at the moment in the [Now](/roadmap/#now) section of my timeline.
